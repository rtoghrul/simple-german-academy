The Basics of Greeting

In German, introducing yourself starts with a friendly greeting followed by your name and origin. Unlike some languages, German formal and informal greetings are quite distinct, but for now, we'll focus on the standard ways used in daily life.

Culture Tip

Germans value direct eye contact during greetings. A firm handshake is standard in professional settings!

Key Phrase

"Freut mich!" (Pleased to meet you) is the perfect follow-up to an introduction.

Grammar Focus: Verbs

Pronoun heißen kommen wohnen sprechen

Du (You) heißt kommst wohnst sprichst

Dialogue: First Encounter

Anna

"Hallo! Ich heiße Anna. Und du?"

Max

"Guten Morgen, Anna. Ich heiße Max. Ich komme aus Berlin."
