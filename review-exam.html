Level C2 Academic Proficiency

Choose the most appropriate nuance for a formal academic debate.

"In Anbetracht der vorliegenden Datenlage lässt sich schlussfolgern, dass die ökonomischen Auswirkungen _________."

Answer Selection Analysis

A) ...nicht zu vernachlässigen sind. Incorrect

Linguistic Explanation: While grammatically sound, this phrase is slightly too common for a highly specialized academic paper. It lacks the precise analytical weight required for C2 level debate.

check Selected & Correct
B) ...einer eingehenden Prüfung bedürfen.

Linguistic Explanation: This choice uses the genitive case ("einer... Prüfung") and the formal verb "bedürfen," which is characteristic of the highest register of German academic writing. It implies a sophisticated necessity for further research.

C) ...ganz schön wichtig sind. Incorrect

Linguistic Explanation: This is colloquial German ("Umgangssprache"). "Ganz schön" should never be used in a formal academic context. It is a stylistic mismatch for the C2 Level.

Linguistic Insight

From Senior Instructor Hans Weber

At the C2 level, German proficiency isn't just about grammar—it's about register consistency. In academic writing (Wissenschaftssprache), we prefer verbs that trigger the genitive case or complex prepositional phrases.

The verb bedürfen is a prime example of a 'High German' construction. Using it correctly demonstrates that you understand not only the meaning of the words but also the social and professional context in which they are deployed.
